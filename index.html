Privacy first

Your conversations belong on your computer.

Flair Messenger does not create a separate account, operate a middleman server or include analytics, ads or tracking. Login and messaging traffic goes directly to official Second Life services through LibreMetaverse.

Read the full privacy policy ↗
01

Encrypted at rest

Remembered settings and message history use Windows DPAPI protection for the current Windows user.

02

No developer-operated server

Your credentials are sent to the official Second Life login service, not to a Flair Messenger server.

03

You stay in control

Close the app and delete its local data folder whenever you want to remove settings and history.
